# 08. App Store Strategy (ASO + Creative)

## Positioning

CVBoosta = ATS Resume Scanner + Tailoring Studio + Career Conversion Intelligence.

## Metadata Draft

- App Name: `CVBoosta: ATS Resume Scanner`
- Subtitle: `Tailor CVs. Boost Interviews.`
- Keywords:
  - `ats resume scanner`
  - `resume checker`
  - `resume tailoring`
  - `cv optimizer`
  - `career ai`
  - `interview prep`

## Screenshot Narrative (6-frame set)

1. Hero: `Your resume is filtered before humans read it.`
2. ATS score: `See exactly what blocks interviews.`
3. Tailoring studio: `Rewrite bullets for role-specific fit.`
4. Tracker: `Turn applications into measurable pipeline.`
5. Analytics: `Track ATS score and interview conversion.`
6. Live activities/widgets: `Stay in flow from lock screen.`

## Icon Concepts

- Concept A: Minimal `C` + upward signal bar on frosted plate
- Concept B: Precision checkmark intersecting document outline
- Concept C: Two-layer card with spark track (subtle, non-gimmicky)

## Onboarding Copy Set

- `Most candidates are rejected by systems first.`
- `Your signal may be the bottleneck, not your ability.`
- `CVBoosta helps your experience get seen.`

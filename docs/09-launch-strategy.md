# 09. Launch Strategy

## GTM Thesis

Lead with painful truth (ATS filtering), then prove short-term win (score increase), then reinforce with behavior loop (streak + tracker).

## Acquisition Channels

- SEO landing pages for role-specific ATS optimization (600+ roles)
- TikTok/Reels short audits: "Why this resume got filtered"
- LinkedIn creator partnerships in career coaching niche
- Product Hunt launch with live demo of scan-to-rewrite workflow

## Conversion Loop

- User scans resume -> sees weak signals -> applies rewrite -> score improves -> shares before/after

## Retention Loop

- Daily streak nudges
- Interview countdown live activities
- Weekly progress summary push

## Launch Phases

- Phase 1: private beta (100 users)
- Phase 2: creator-backed waitlist onboarding
- Phase 3: public App Store release + paid search
- Phase 4: role intelligence expansion + B2B recruiter insights
